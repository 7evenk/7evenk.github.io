Plan with picture cards

Drag a card onto a time, choose the destination calendar and save in Google’s small dialog. Immediate saving is optional and off by default.

🌤️

Weather has its own space

Optional hourly weather occupies a separate lane beside events. Sunrise, sunset and a daylight gradient add context for planning.

👨‍👩‍👧

Use familiar calendars

Use separate Google calendars for people or shared activities. Google continues to handle times, recurrence, sharing and synchronisation.

What the extension adds

39 picture cards, custom cards with names, emoji and an optional local image, large symbols in recognised day/week views, and German/English controls. The card palette reserves space beside the calendar, or below it in narrow windows.

What about phones?

Saved emoji and titles also appear in mobile Google Calendar apps. Large symbols, the picture-card palette and weather decoration are added by this desktop Chrome extension only in the browser. It is not a smartphone app.

Know the limits

The extension works with Google’s visible calendar interface. Google changes may require compatibility updates. Custom images stay in this browser profile and are not uploaded to Google. Weather covers the available forecast period only; calculated daylight is not a weather forecast.
