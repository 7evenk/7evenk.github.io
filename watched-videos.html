The problem, solved

Your playlists should not become a graveyard.

YouTube offers limited cleanup controls. This extension checks the visible watch progress, keeps unfinished videos, and removes only the entries that meet your chosen threshold.

Three simple steps

From cluttered to clean.

01

Open a playlist

Use Watch Later or one of your own YouTube playlists.

02

Choose your threshold

Set how much of a video counts as watched. The safe default is 100%.

03

Review and clean

Confirm once, follow the progress, and cancel safely whenever needed.

Frequently asked

Good to know.

Does it work with Watch Later?

Yes. Watch Later is supported, along with playlists you can edit.

Will it delete partially watched videos?

Only videos at or above your selected threshold are removed. With the default 100%, a video at 42% stays in your playlist.

Why does a large playlist take time?

YouTube loads playlist entries gradually. The extension scrolls automatically and shows how many entries have loaded before cleanup begins.

Can I stop the cleanup?

Yes. The progress card includes a cancel button, and cancellation stops before the next removal.

Does it collect my YouTube history?

No. There is no account, analytics service, or transfer of your viewing activity.
